# Exclusive owner and loan epochs (abstract model)

This is an initial formal ownership model for a single allocation, two possible owning places and two loan slots. It models allocation, `take!`, `drop!`, read and mutable `borrow!`, release, and admission of owner/loan handles. These are abstract atomic steps, not a compiler or concurrency implementation. Only one live owner place is represented; a writer loan excludes all other loans; a live loan prevents transfer/retirement. Transfers, retirement and allocation advance an unbounded mathematical owner generation; each loan slot advances its epoch upon reissue. Opaque capability authenticity and the absence of integer wrap are assumptions, not conclusions.

The invariant is inductive for every modeled state transition. In addition to initialization and preservation, the model establishes monotonically increasing/nondecreasing epochs, stale-generation rejection, no owner write alongside a live loan, no owner read alongside a writer loan and no access after retirement. Four counterexample controls demonstrate why mutable-loan exclusivity, retirement fencing, generation advancement and slot epoch advancement matter. Five satisfiable controls show that the admission conditions are not all false.
